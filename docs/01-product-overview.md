# PropertyFlow — Product Overview

> One platform to run condos, HOAs and rental communities: residents, money, maintenance, security and governance in one place.

---

## 1. What is PropertyFlow?

PropertyFlow is a web + mobile platform for managing residential communities.
It replaces spreadsheets, email threads, paper logbooks and separate tools with **one system** that managers, boards, staff and residents all use.

It is modelled on **Property Control** (the company behind Condo Control, HOA Central, HOA Sites, Patrol Points and Condo Manager).

---

## 2. Who is it for?

| User | What they need | What PropertyFlow gives them |
|---|---|---|
| **Property management companies** | Manage many communities with a small team | Multi-property dashboard, automation, reports |
| **Property / community managers** | Stop chasing emails and paper | One inbox for requests, tasks, bookings, payments |
| **Board members** | Oversight without meetings for everything | Board portal, approvals, e-voting, financial reports |
| **Residents (owners & tenants)** | Quick answers and self-service | Mobile portal: pay, book, request, get notified |
| **Front desk / security staff** | Fast logging at the desk | Packages, visitors, incidents, patrols, keys |
| **Vendors / contractors** | Clear work orders and faster payment | Vendor portal for jobs and invoices |
| **Self-managed HOAs** | Run the community with volunteers | Simple setup, same tools, no management company needed |

**Community types:** condominiums, HOAs, co-ops, apartment/rental buildings, mixed-use. Size: 5 units to thousands.

---

## 3. Features

### Community setup
- Companies → Communities → Buildings → Units
- Resident database (owners, tenants, occupants, vehicles, pets)
- Roles & permissions per community

### Communication
- Announcements & notices (email, SMS, push, in-app)
- Event calendar
- Discussion forum & classifieds
- Surveys & polls
- Staff / contact phone book

### Maintenance
- Service requests with photos, status and chat
- Work orders assigned to staff or vendors
- Preventive (scheduled) maintenance
- Community asset inventory
- Task tracking with due dates

### Amenities
- Book gym, party room, pool, guest suite, etc.
- Rules: time slots, limits, fees, deposits, approval

### Security & front desk
- Package tracking with resident notification
- Visitor log & guest passes
- Visitor parking permits
- Incident reports
- Key tracking (sign-in / sign-out)
- Security patrols with checkpoints (QR/NFC)
- Authorized entry log

### Finance
- Monthly dues / rent / fees generated automatically
- Online payments (card, bank)
- Resident ledger & statements
- Late fees and reminders
- Vendor invoices & approval flow
- Budgets and financial reports
- Export to accounting (QuickBooks, CSV)

### Governance
- Rule violations with notices and fines
- Architectural change requests (approve / reject)
- E-voting, proxy voting, weighted voting
- Electronic consent forms
- Virtual meetings / AGM records
- Document library (bylaws, minutes, budgets)

### Portals & apps
- Manager dashboard (web)
- Board portal
- Resident portal (web + mobile-friendly / PWA, REST API for native apps)
- Vendor portal
- Public community website

### AI assistant
- Answers routine resident questions from community documents
- Drafts announcements and notices
- Summarises financials and builds board reports

## 4. How it adds value

| Problem today | With PropertyFlow |
|---|---|
| Requests lost in email and phone calls | Every request tracked from open to closed |
| Residents call the office for simple things | Self-service: pay, book, request, download 24/7 |
| Late or missed payments | Auto billing, reminders, online pay → faster cash |
| Paper logbooks at the front desk | Digital logs, searchable, instant notifications |
| Boards wait weeks for decisions | Online approvals and e-voting in days |
| Managers use 5–10 different tools | One login, one database, one bill |
| No clear picture across properties | Live dashboards and reports per community |
| Hard to grow a management company | Handle more doors with the same team |

**In one line:** less manual work, clearer money, better communication, happier residents.

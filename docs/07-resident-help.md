# PropertyFlow — Resident Help

> A guide for residents (owners, tenants and occupants) using your community's PropertyFlow
> portal. If you manage the community instead of living there, see `06-admin-guide.md`.

---

## Getting started

You'll receive a link from your property manager or board to set up your login — there's no
public sign-up, since your community adds you first. Once you're in, everything for your unit
(and any others you're connected to) is in one place.

---

## Your dashboard

Shows the homes you're currently connected to. If you've lived in more than one unit over time
(moved, or owned then sold), your past residencies stay on your record too, but only your
*current* home shows on the dashboard.

---

## Staying informed

- **Announcements**: shows notices for your building/unit and anything community-wide. Pinned
  ones stay at the top.
- **Documents**: anything your community has shared with you — bylaws, rules, meeting minutes,
  forms — organized into folders. What you can see depends on whether you're an owner or tenant;
  some documents are owners-only.
- **Events**: the community calendar. RSVP if asked to.
- **Notifications**: the bell icon collects everything — new announcements, package arrivals,
  request updates, and more. Turn categories on or off in Settings → Notifications.

---

## Getting help

**Submit a service request** for anything that needs fixing, with photos if helpful. You'll see
its status change as staff pick it up, and can follow along with comments. **Report an incident**
to security/front desk if something happened that they should know about.

---

## Booking amenities

Pick an amenity (gym, party room, pool, guest suite — whatever your community offers), see open
time slots, and book. Some amenities need manager approval before they're confirmed, and some
charge a fee or require a deposit — you'll see that up front, before you book. Cancel ahead of the
cancellation window if your plans change.

---

## Packages, visitors & parking

- **Packages**: you'll be notified the moment one arrives for you at the front desk, and again if
  it sits uncollected. Sign for it when you pick it up.
- **Guest passes**: issue a redeemable code for a visitor ahead of time, so front desk can let them
  in without calling you.
- **Parking permits**: request a visitor parking permit for your unit (limited to a few active at
  once — ask your manager about your community's limit).

---

## Paying your bills

See your unit's invoices and running balance any time, plus a downloadable statement. Pay online
where your community has it enabled (a real payment provider is coming later — for now this may
be running in a test mode your manager can tell you about), or your manager will record payments
made another way.

---

## Having your say

If your community holds **ballots** (formal votes) or **meetings**, you'll see them here — vote
if you're an owner, or follow along either way. Owners can also submit **renovation/architectural
requests** for approval before starting work on their unit, respond to **surveys and polls**, and
sign **consent forms** digitally when asked. The **community board** is an open discussion space
for residents — check your community's rules before posting.

If you believe you've received a **bylaw notice** in error, or want to understand one, reach out
to your management team — notices and any fines are tracked on your record and visible to you.

---

## Your privacy

You're always in control of your own information. From Settings → Profile, you can ask that your
personal data (name, email, phone) be erased. Your management team reviews the request — once
approved, your identifying details are removed right away. Your history (past residencies,
invoices, votes) stays on record for the community's own accounting and governance needs, but is
no longer tied to your name.

---

## Something not working?

Check with your property manager or board first — they can see and fix most account issues
(access, unit assignment, notification problems) directly. If the whole site seems to be down,
it's worth trying again in a few minutes before reaching out.
